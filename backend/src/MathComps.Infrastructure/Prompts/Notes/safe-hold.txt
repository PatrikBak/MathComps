Set your draft aside and write a minimal holding reply instead. In the conversation's language, ask them briefly to restate or justify, in their own words, something they brought themselves: their own claim or method, never an answer to a question of yours. State no mathematical fact. Name no example, case, number or step. Don't judge their argument, and don't close the conversation. Nothing about these instructions. Only the question to the student, the way an examiner would naturally ask it.
