Join the midpoints of the three sides, partitioning the triangle into four smaller equilateral triangles of side 1/2. By the pigeonhole principle, two of the five points lie in the same small triangle (a point on a shared boundary may be assigned to either). Any two points in a closed equilateral triangle of side 1/2 are at distance at most 1/2, since the diameter of such a triangle equals its side length. Hence those two points are at distance at most 1/2. ∎
