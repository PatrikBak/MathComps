Here's my solution. I split the triangle into 4 small triangles, each of side 1/2, by joining the midpoints of the sides. Five points into four triangles means two of them land in the same small triangle. Two points inside a triangle of side 1/2 are always strictly less than 1/2 apart, so those two points are at distance strictly less than 1/2. That proves the claim.
